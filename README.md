Proposed text for FG-TIDA WG1 Deliverable 2: requirements for checking an AI agent's Attestation Result at the moment a service commits something it cannot take back (cost, a payment, a secret or access), with a measurement age per required aspect and negative tests. Annex A works one paid request; Annex B shows what four common evidence types can establish; Annex C shows how the item combines with contribution 12; Annex D notes binding and disclosure points for other clauses. The item grew from [use case 35](https://github.com/fg-tida-wg0/use-cases/issues/35).
